# Noah Garrett

I build software for construction commissioning and QA/QC teams. I work at LotusWorks, and most of the projects here started as a spreadsheet or PDF problem at work: equipment lists, inspection logs, drawing sets and timesheets that someone was reconciling by hand every week.

I also run Visualize Build LLC, where I make [Nightglass](https://night.glass), a PDF and document editor for Mac, Windows and Linux that runs on your own computer. I built it for the files I deal with every day. It stays fast on huge construction drawing sets, edits the actual text in a PDF, does measurements and quantity takeoffs, compares revisions and redacts for good. There's an optional private AI assistant, no account to sign up for, and your documents stay on your machine.

## Projects

- [Multitool](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/Multitool): 15 office tools (PDF markup, OCR, forms, diagrams, dashboards) in one HTML file. [Live](https://noahfgarrett.github.io/Multitool/)
- [PowerBI-Custom-Visuals](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/PowerBI-Custom-Visuals): 40 Power BI custom visuals in a TypeScript monorepo, from general charts to QA/QC dashboards
- [Recreation](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/Recreation): offline desktop app that works out how P&ID and electrical drawing sheets connect, using PDF text and OCR
- [KPIntelligence](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/kpintelligence): desktop app that turns synced SharePoint spreadsheets into dashboards, PDF reports and PowerPoint decks
- [Risk-Analysis](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/Risk-Analysis): scores commissioning equipment workbooks for step coverage and issue risk, all in the browser
- [FinanceReconciliation](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/FinanceReconciliation): reconciles payroll hours exports against timesheet PDFs and produces client invoices
- [LunchCards](https://github.com/noahfgarrett/LunchCards): the card game we play at lunch. [Live](https://noahfgarrett.github.io/LunchCards/)
